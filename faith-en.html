Our Doctrine & Beliefs

Statement of Faith - LCAC

We firmly believe and confess the foundational biblical truths and sound apostolic teaching.

Click on any card to jump directly to its scripture reference
01

The Holy Bible

Infallible, divinely inspired by God, and the supreme authority for Christian faith and life without addition or alteration.

View Scripture (NIV)
02

The Trinity

One God in three persons (Father, Son, and Holy Spirit), co-equal in eternity and power, the Creator and Sustainer of all things.

View Scripture (NIV)
03

God the Father

Eternal, spiritual, omnipotent, omniscient, good, and loving, who gave His Son for the redemption of humanity.

View Scripture (NIV)
04

Jesus Christ

The eternal Son, the Word incarnate born of the Virgin Mary, true God and true man, sinless, died on the cross for redemption, rose, ascended, and will return in glory.

View Scripture (NIV)
05

The Holy Spirit

The third person, Giver of life, convicts the world, dwells in believers, produces fruit in them, and empowers them with sanctification and gifts.

View Scripture (NIV)
06

Humanity

Created in the image of God, fell through transgression (original sin), separated from God, and utterly helpless without Jesus Christ.

View Scripture (NIV)
07

Satan

A fallen angel seeking to destroy humanity, disarmed by Christ’s death, and destined for eternal punishment.

View Scripture (NIV)
08

Salvation

By grace alone through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ; new birth, justification, sanctification, and eternal life.

View Scripture (NIV)
09

The Church

The community of "born-again" believers, whose mission is to preach the Gospel, make disciples, and rescue the lost.

View Scripture (NIV)
10

The Ordinances

Including believer's baptism (by full immersion as an identity declaration and union with Christ’s death and resurrection) and Holy Communion.

View Scripture (NIV)
11

Baptism in the Holy Spirit

An experience empowering believers with supernatural strength and spiritual gifts to be effective witnesses.

View Scripture (NIV)
12

Gifts and Fruit of the Spirit

Supernatural gifts for building up the church, and the fruit of the Spirit as the natural result of a life led by the Spirit.

View Scripture (NIV)
13

Divine Healing

Available for spirit, soul, and body through the atoning sacrifice of Jesus Christ.

View Scripture (NIV)
14

Prayer, Resurrection & Prophecy

Prayer is communion with God; there is a bodily resurrection, and the church ages will culminate in Christ's visible return.

View Scripture (NIV)
